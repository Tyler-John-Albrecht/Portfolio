Honest answers

What people ask before booking

Do you need to use my water and electricity?
No. The truck carries its own water tank and power, so nothing runs off your house. That's exactly why we can work at offices, apartment complexes and storage lots where there's no spigot in sight.
Do I have to be home while you work?
Not at all, and plenty of customers aren't. Leave it unlocked or leave a key and we'll text you photos when it's done. If you'd rather do the walk-around in person, we'll book a time you're around.
What happens if it rains?
This is Florida, so it comes up. If there's covered parking — a garage, a carport, a covered space at your building — we usually work straight through. If not, we move you to the next opening at no charge and you keep your place in line.
How long does it take?
The Reset runs about two hours. The Full Detail is four to five. Correction with a ceramic coating is a one-to-two day job, because the coating cures on its own clock. You get a real window when you book, not "sometime Tuesday."
Can you come to my office or apartment complex?
Yes, as long as we can park next to the vehicle for the duration. Office lots are some of our busiest bookings — you go to work dirty and drive home clean. For apartments and HOAs, just check that on-site washing is allowed.
Do you really do boats, RVs and motorcycles?
We do, and it's a real part of the business rather than a line on a menu. Boats on the trailer or in the slip, motorhomes wherever they're stored, bikes done entirely by hand with no pressure washer near the chrome. Send a photo and the length and we'll price it.
What's the difference between this and a $30 car wash?
Everything. A wash rinses the surface. A detail is decontamination, machine correction, interior extraction and protection — done by hand, under lighting that shows every flaw. A wash lasts a week. A detail you feel for months.
How do I pay?
Card, tap, cash or Zelle — after the walk-around, once you've seen the work. Nothing up front and no deposit on standard details.
